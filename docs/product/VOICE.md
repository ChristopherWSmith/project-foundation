# Voice — __PROJECT_NAME__

## Principles (same across the portfolio)

1. **Plain over clever.** Short words, short sentences. Say the thing.
2. **Honest over hype.** No "revolutionary", no "seamless", no promises the product can't keep.
3. **Respect the reader's time.** Lead with what they need to know or do.
4. **Calm under failure.** Errors say what happened, whether their data is safe, and what to do next. Never blame the user.
5. **AI is labeled.** Anything generated by AI says so, and invites review where it matters.

## Rules

- **Product name:** always written **__PROJECT_NAME__**. Never abbreviated, never in all caps. Spelling is checked by `brand/copy-lint.json`.
- **Capitalization:** sentence case everywhere: buttons, headings, menus.
- **Person:** "you" for the user; "we" sparingly, never "the user".
- **Numbers:** numerals ("3 accounts"); money with currency formatting from the user's locale.
- **Banned words:** kept in `brand/copy-lint.json`, which CI warns on.

## Examples (these matter more than the rules)

| Situation | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| Primary button | Save changes | Submit |
| Error, our fault | Something went wrong on our side. Your changes are safe — try again in a minute. | Error 500: Internal Server Error |
| Error, user can fix | That email is already in use. Sign in instead? | Invalid input |
| Empty state | No transactions yet. Connect an account to see them here. | No data |
| Destructive confirm | Delete "Q3 budget"? This can't be undone. | Are you sure? |
| Success | Budget saved. | Success! |
| Email subject | Your weekly summary is ready | 🚀 Exciting news inside!!! |
| AI output label | Suggested by AI — review before saving | (no label) |

## Where copy lives

UI strings live in one place (the strings / locale file), not scattered through components, so they can be reviewed, linted and translated.
